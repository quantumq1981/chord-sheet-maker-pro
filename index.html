📖 Getting Started & Syntax Guide — tap to open
Quick start (3 steps)
  1. Get a chart in: type chords in the editor (see syntax below), or tap Import to load a Guitar Pro, MusicXML, PDF, ChordPro, or Ultimate Guitar file.
  2. Style it: use Settings for bars-per-row, fonts and chord spellings; use Transpose (▲▼) to change key.
  3. Get it out: Print (best on iPad/iPhone → "Save to PDF"), or ↓ PDF / ↓ PNG / Copy.
Editing syntax — CSMPN Fake Book (the main editor)
  • Header lines: Title: Composer: Key: Time: Tempo: Capo: — one per line.
  • One chord = one bar. Split a bar with _ → Bb7_A7. % repeats the previous bar; N.C. = no chord.
  • Sections: - Verse (plain), : Chorus (boxed rehearsal mark), = Bridge. Add a rehearsal letter after the name if you like.
  • Repeats & endings: |: … :| or (A B C D)x2; first/second endings: 1. C D 2. E F.
  • Accents on a chord: ! accent, ^ marcato, . staccato (e.g. G!).
  • Lyrics / notes: lines starting with ; are treated as lyric/comment lines.
Importing other formats
  • Guitar Pro (.gp .gp3 .gp4 .gp5 .gpx) — auto-converts to a fake-book chart, with TAB voicings where present.
  • MusicXML (.xml .musicxml .mxl) — extracts chord symbols and structure from an engraved score.
  • Ultimate Guitar — paste UG text, or import a UG Pro PDF (the standard-notation + tab hybrid layout).
  • ChordPro, ABC, OnSong, OpenSong, OpenLyrics, iReal Pro, ChordMark — all available from the Import menu.
  • After any import, open Import Details (power mode) to see what was detected.
ChordSlashML — beat-level rhythm editor (advanced)
  • Open the ChordSlashML editor for a live two-pane view: type on the left, see slash notation on the right.
  • Uses [Section] labels and beat-slotted measures (C _ _ _, Am _ G _) with |: :| repeats.
  • Load into Chart converts it to a CSMPN fake-book chart (or a notated rhythm chart when Slash-Rhythm View is on). ⬆ From Source goes the other way.
Exporting
  • Editor: Print (iOS → Save to PDF), ↓ PDF, ↓ PNG, Copy.
  • Slash Notation & ChordSlashML panels add ↓ SVG, ↓ MusicXML (.xml, opens in notation apps) and ↓ LilyPond.
  • Setlist can print several saved charts to one PDF.
Keyboard shortcuts (external keyboard)
  • Ctrl/⌘ + S = Save · Ctrl/⌘ + P = Print · Ctrl/⌘ + ↑/↓ = Transpose.

★ ChordSlashML — Stage-Ready Sheet Guide

ChordSlashML uses pipe-delimited measures where every beat slot is visible. Each step below adds one layer — click Try this → to load the example into the live editor and see it render instantly.

1

Start with a complete header

Title, key, time, and tempo appear above the staff on every export — SVG, MusicXML, and print. A complete header is the difference between a draft and a stage-ready sheet.

Title: My Song
Composer: Your Name
Key: C
Time: 4/4
Tempo: 120
2

Add section labels

[Section] labels render as bold boxed rehearsal marks above the staff — exactly what musicians scan for on stage. Use standard names: Intro, Verse, Pre-Chorus, Chorus, Bridge, Outro.

Title: My Song
Key: C
Time: 4/4
Tempo: 120

[Verse]
| C _ _ _ | Am _ _ _ | F _ _ _ | G _ _ _ |

[Chorus]
| F _ _ _ | G _ _ _ | C _ _ _ | C _ _ _ |
3

Place chord changes mid-bar

When two chords share a bar, put the second chord where it lands rhythmically. In 4/4, | Am _ G _ | means Am on beat 1, G on beat 3 — exactly two beats each. The underscore _ is a slash beat: hold the chord.

Title: Autumn Leaves
Key: Gm
Time: 4/4
Tempo: 130

[A]
| Cm7 _ F7 _ | Bbmaj7 _ _ _ | Ebmaj7 _ _ _ | Am7b5 _ D7 _ |
| Gm _ _ _ | Gm _ _ _ | Am7b5 _ D7 _ | Gm _ _ _ |
4

Mark repeated sections with barlines

|: … :| wraps any repeated section. Performers read this instantly — no need to write the section twice. Pair with section labels for maximum clarity.

Title: My Song
Key: G
Time: 4/4
Tempo: 120

[Verse]
| G _ _ _ | Em _ _ _ | C _ _ _ | D _ _ _ |

[Chorus]
|: G _ _ _ | C _ _ _ | D _ _ _ | G _ _ _ :|
5

Full stage-ready chart

Combine everything: complete header, section labels, mid-bar changes, and repeat barlines. This is the format that produces the cleanest, most readable SVG and MusicXML output for rehearsal, recording, and performance.

Title: Autumn Leaves
Composer: J. Kosma / J. Mercer
Key: Gm
Time: 4/4
Tempo: 130

[Intro]
| Gm _ _ _ | Gm _ _ _ ||

[A — Head]
| Cm7 _ F7 _ | Bbmaj7 _ _ _ | Ebmaj7 _ _ _ | Am7b5 _ D7 _ |
| Gm _ _ _ | Gm _ _ _ | Am7b5 _ D7 _ | Gm _ _ _ |

[B — Bridge]
|: Cm7 _ F7 _ | Bbmaj7 _ _ _ | Ebmaj7 _ _ _ | Ebmaj7 _ _ _ :|

[Outro]
| Am7b5 _ D7 _ | Gm _ _ _ | Gm _ _ _ | Gm _ _ _ ||
